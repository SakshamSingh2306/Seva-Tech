About this model: a real 5-layer CNN (~2.9M parameters), trained from scratch and exported to ONNX. This particular checkpoint was trained on a small 10-class, 100-images-per-class slice of PlantVillage for 10 epochs on a single CPU core (~55% validation accuracy) — enough to prove the pipeline genuinely learns, not a placeholder. It recognizes the 10 classes listed below, not the full 38-class PlantVillage set.
